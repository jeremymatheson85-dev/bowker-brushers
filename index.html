For kids aged 3 to 8

Two minutes, twice a day, made fun.

Sparkle Buddies turns toothbrushing into a game kids actually want to play. A buddy shows them exactly where to brush next, and every brush moves them across a magical kingdom.

Brushing, one step at a time

Pick a buddy, press start, and brush along.

1

Pick a buddy

Each child chooses an animal buddy of their own, who brushes right along with them.

2

Brush along

A mouth map lights up where to go next: top, bottom, left, right, outside, inside and the chewing tops.

3

Move forward

Every brush takes a step across the kingdom, with gems, treasure chests and surprises along the way.

Made for families

Safe by design

Sparkle Buddies works fully without an account, and in that mode nothing leaves your device. Accounts, settings and deleting data all sit behind a parental gate.
