More help & support ↗
Is Sparkle Buddies really free?

Yes. There are no in-app purchases and no ads. Buddies earn their rewards through brushing, not spending.

What ages is it for?

Sparkle Buddies is designed for children aged 3–8. Younger brushers get a grown-up turn built into the session, and the brushing level can change as they become more independent.

Can my children share a device?

Yes! Each child can have their own profile, buddy, and brushing progress on the same device.

Do we need to be patients at Bowker Creek Dental?

No. Any family can enjoy the brushing adventures. The clinic’s real-world prize tickets are an extra for Bowker Creek Dental families.

Which devices can we use?

Download Sparkle Buddies from the App Store for iPhone and iPad. An optional account lets you sync your family’s progress between devices.
